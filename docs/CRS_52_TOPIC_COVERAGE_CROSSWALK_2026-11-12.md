## Changes in this pass

- Expanded Asa's reforms, the Twelve and the Seventy/Seventy-two, Wedding Feast/garment, Dragnet and adoption/joint heirship.
- Strengthened lessons 33–36 with biblical narratives, explanatory comparisons, worked examples and checks.
- Added dedicated sections on providence, parenting, obedience and decision-making, corruption, and prescribed sexual-ethics passages.
- Existing content and lesson IDs were retained; this work is on the preview only.

## Remaining release gates

- Manually verify each prescribed passage, subtopic and objective against the complete IBASS document, not just the 52 headings.
- Scripture fact-check all lesson narratives and references, including Gospel differences and translations.
- Run repository tests and browser-level checks for topic dropdowns, lesson rendering, mapped past questions, and UI parity.
- Only then deploy the approved preview to production and verify the live app.
