# JAMB Syllabus-to-Lesson Coverage Audit — 5 November 2026

## Scope and authority
Compared the current **preview** lessons against JAMB's official IBASS Government, Economics and Commerce syllabuses, with emphasis on the detailed topic/contents and learning objectives, not just the number of lessons.

Official sources:
- Government: https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/assets/uploads/Government.pdf
- Economics: https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/assets/uploads/Economics.pdf
- Commerce: https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/assets/uploads/Commerce.pdf

This is a **targeted syllabus gap audit and correction pass**, not a certification that every official sub-objective has been manually tested against every sentence, example and question. Absence of an exact phrase was used to flag items for substantive review; it is not conclusive evidence of absence of the underlying concept.

Every original lesson ID occurs in the respective preview, every original lesson has at least one mapped question key, and no mapped key points to a non-existent lesson ID. These are **structural** checks, not proof that each question's classification or answer is correct.

## Confirmed coverage and important qualifications

**Government:** The 17 lessons span political concepts and systems, organs, constitutions, rights, elections, public administration, Nigerian political history, federalism, foreign policy and international organisations. JAMB's syllabus has more granular contents and objectives within those areas. A targeted text review found insufficient explicit treatment of political socialisation and public-service commissions; both were supplemented.

**Economics:** The 20 lessons cover broad economics foundations, tools, demand/supply, consumer theory, production, market structures, national income, money, finance, growth, agriculture, population, international trade and organisations. Official syllabus details explicitly include inductive/deductive reasoning, isoquant-isocost, short-run shutdown, cross elasticity, Fisher quantity equation, trade creation/diversion and instability of agricultural income. These were supplemented after a targeted review.

**Commerce:** All 16 broad blocks of the official Commerce syllabus have plausible matching lesson groups among the 54 app lessons: commerce; occupation; production; trade; purchase/sale; aids-to-trade; business units; financing; trade associations; money; stock exchange; management; marketing; legal aspects; ICT; business environment/social responsibility. The official syllabus includes detailed terms not sufficiently explicit in the preview: consular invoice, luncheon/fuel vouchers, NDLEA, second-tier securities market, bureau de change, matrix structure, e-banking and countertrade. These were supplemented.

## Changes made — preview only

Government:
- `concepts`: political socialisation, political culture and participation.
- `public-administration`: public service commission versus public corporation.

Economics:
- `tools-analysis`: inductive and deductive reasoning.
- `elasticity`: cross-price elasticity with calculation.
- `production`: isoquant/isocost and numerical example.
- `market-structures`: break-even and short-run shutdown.
- `money-inflation`: Fisher quantity equation and worked example.
- `agriculture`: agricultural income instability and responses.
- `international-trade`: trade creation versus trade diversion.

Commerce:
- `trade-documents-payments`: consular invoice.
- `modern-retailing-channels`: luncheon/fuel vouchers.
- `consumer-business-environment`: NDLEA and other product-safety regulators.
- `capital-market`: historical second-tier securities market.
- `business-finance`: bureau de change.
- `business-management`: matrix structure.
- `communication-ict`: e-banking versus e-commerce and e-business.
- `foreign-trade-documents`: countertrade.

## Remaining assurance checks before production

1. Create a line-by-line matrix for **each official JAMB subtopic and learning objective**, marking exact lesson section and evidence. Topic-level alignment alone is not a full certification.
2. Review Government's Nigerian constitutional and pre-colonial subtopics individually, and check historical accuracy and syllabus version.
3. Verify Economics mathematical and graphical objectives with representative solved problems and diagrams, especially indifference curves, isoquants, market equilibrium, multipliers, and measures of dispersion.
4. Verify Commerce legal and financial terminology against the era of each historical question; do not present superseded historical rules as current law.
5. Execute browser-level past-question loading and diagram rendering for every lesson; mapping records alone do not establish runtime success.
6. Validate the precise **current** IBASS revision before release; some publicly reproduced syllabuses include later edits not present in the downloadable IBASS PDF, so resolve differences explicitly.

## Release decision

**Preview updated; production unchanged.** No claim of 100% syllabus-objective coverage or complete question-level QA is warranted until the remaining checks are finished.
