# Commerce 2009–2012: source and answer review

Four supplied papers, 50 original questions each. This adds 200 questions to the published 2001–2008 bank. Original printed keys, wording and option counts are preserved, including missing-option defects. No official JAMB marking scheme was established.

New-paper academic classifications: {'corrected': 20, 'multiple': 16, 'noanswer': 4, 'misprint': 8, 'debatable': 11}; plus 3 preserved administrative booklet-identification instructions. All questions have a prepared offline Teach Me and a Key Points mapping. The combined bank has 42 lessons and 36 reviewed repeat groups (92 occurrences). These lessons cover the imported topics; this is not a claim of a completed full-syllabus audit.

[Independent audit](commerce-2009-2012-audit.md) · [Lesson coverage](commerce-2009-2012-coverage.md) · [Repeated-question rationale](commerce-2001-2012-repeats.md)

## Source safeguards

- The three booklet-identification questions (2010–2012 Q1) are preserved as administrative instructions. They have no universal answer, credit any attempt, and are excluded from defective-question study counts and fast games. Their asterisks are not invented answer keys.
- Commerce 2011 Q11 is a substantive retailer question whose compiled key is an asterisk; D, breaking bulk, is explained as the answer without claiming an omitted key was a wrong letter.
- Commerce 2005 Q39 and Commerce 2007 Q26 have only three printed options. No fourth option was invented.
- The 2005 Q39 compiled key says C/D, but no D is supplied. C is accepted and the source-key defect is explained.
- Source typos and conflicting figures are retained and explained; historical institutional/legal references are taught in context.
- Corrected answers enter Wrong Answers in Circulation; genuine multiple and missing-correct-option cases enter the corresponding Tricky Questions category. Only real distractor errors become Exam Traps. Repeated tested points retain their separate year occurrences.
- Fast games exclude multiple/no-correct-option questions. Defective-option questions credit any attempt without painting an offered answer factually correct.
- Published CRS metadata and content remain preserved; no shared backend configuration changes. The 45-second content download timeout remains.

## Reviewed answer and wording cases

### commerce-2009-q7

A chain store usually combines the features of

A. multiple shops and departmental stores
B. hypermarkets and stalls
C. multiple shops and hypermarkets
D. mail order business and multiple shops

Printed key: **A**. Treatment: **debatable**. Accepted: **A**.

A is the intended classroom combination of centrally controlled multiple outlets and organized departmental retailing. However, a chain need not contain departments; common ownership of several outlets is the essential chain feature.

### commerce-2009-q10

The most commonly used commercial documents are

A. order notes, debit notes, invoices and quotations
B. letters of enquiry, consignment notes, receipts and invoices
C. credit notes, statement of accounts, dispatch order and bills
D. balance sheet, receipts, invoices and manifests

Printed key: **A**. Treatment: **debatable**. Accepted: **A**.

A is the supplied representative set of routine commercial documents: orders, debit notes, invoices and quotations. Other offered lists also contain real documents, so “most commonly” should not be taught as an exclusive universal ranking.

### commerce-2009-q11

The term free on board means that the price includes

A. cost and insurance only
B. costs and freight only
C. freight and insurance only
D. cost, insurance and freight

Printed key: **D**. Treatment: **noanswer**. Accepted: **No fully correct option; credit any attempt**.

No offered option correctly defines FOB. Free on board normally places delivery and risk transfer when goods are loaded on the buyer-nominated vessel at the shipment port; it does not require the seller to include main ocean freight and insurance as CIF does.

- [International Trade Administration: Know Your Incoterms](https://www.trade.gov/know-your-incoterms)

### commerce-2009-q13

A cheque which a bank draws on its own funds is a

A. bank draft
B. cashier's cheque

Printed key: **A**. Treatment: **multiple**. Accepted: **A, B, C**.

A bank draft, cashier’s cheque and bank cheque can all describe bank-issued instruments drawn on bank funds in this broad wording. The stem does not identify a drawee arrangement that distinguishes A, B and C. Accept all three.

### commerce-2009-q24

### commerce-2009-q31

Use the Information below to answer questions 31 and 32. Ojo’s Balance Sheet as at 31st December, 2006.
Liabilities/equity: Capital 10,000; Drawings 600; Balance 9,400; Profit 6,200; Balance 15,600; Creditors 1,800; Total 17,400.
Assets: Land and building 12,000; Fittings 850; Motor van 1,600; Cash 2,150; Prepayments 700; Debtors 100; Total 17,400.
10% is charged on depreciation of fixed assets.

Determine the value of Ojo's fixed assets.

A. ₦14 450
B. ₦13 450
C. ₦13 005
D. ₦12 005.

Printed key: **A**. Treatment: **corrected**. Accepted: **C**.

Fixed assets before depreciation total ₦12,000 + ₦850 + ₦1,600 = ₦14,450. Applying the stated 10% depreciation gives ₦14,450 × 90% = ₦13,005, C. Printed A is the pre-depreciation amount.

### commerce-2009-q37

The major dealers on the stock market are the

A. agents
B. jobbers
C. investors
D. brokers

Printed key: **D**. Treatment: **multiple**. Accepted: **D, B**.

Retain printed D, brokers, as major professional participants executing stock-market transactions. The stem says only major dealers and does not specify own-account trading. Do not declare brokers factually wrong by adding that missing restriction.

### commerce-2009-q43

Price skimming is a pricing policy that begins with a relatively high price, often for a new differentiated product, and may reduce the price as the market develops. The source says “Marketing skimming,” intending price or market skimming.

### commerce-2010-q16

The document issued to a port authority when goods are deposited is a?

A. dock landing account
B. bill of sight
C. bill of lading
D. dock warrant

Printed key: **D**. Treatment: **misprint**. Accepted: **D**.

### commerce-2010-q17

The details of the goods required by the purchaser is outlined in?

A. a consular invoice
B. an indent
C. an invoice
D. a certificate of origin

Printed key: **C**. Treatment: **corrected**. Accepted: **B**.

An indent specifies the goods an importer or purchaser requires and directs their procurement. An invoice instead records a seller's charge for supplied goods; a certificate of origin identifies their origin.

### commerce-2010-q24

A major hindrance to tourism growth in Nigeria is?

B. lack of tourism master plan

Printed key: **B**. Treatment: **corrected**. Accepted: **C**.

### commerce-2010-q27

An arrangement by independent firms to share the market of their products on quota basis is referred to as?

A. trust
B. integration
C. syndicate
D. cartel

Printed key: **C**. Treatment: **corrected**. Accepted: **D**.

A cartel is an arrangement among independent firms to coordinate competition, including market shares or output quotas. A syndicate is a broader cooperative arrangement for a particular transaction or activity and does not specifically identify quota-based market allocation.
