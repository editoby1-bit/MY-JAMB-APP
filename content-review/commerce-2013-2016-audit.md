## Resolved question checks

- Physical effort does not establish unskilled work. 2013 Q2, 2015 Q4 and 2016 Q12 accept C, blue-collar. Retrieved Cambridge terminology and BLS masonry training/strength requirements support the manual/skill distinction.
- 2013 Q6 uses C, balance of trade, for net trading position; desire to trade is not the measure. Q9 uses A, consular invoice, rather than a certificate of origin. Q31 uses planning C for the initial management function.
- 2013 Q15: insured value ₦25,000 / actual value ₦100,000 × loss ₦30,000 = ₦7,500. Q16’s deliberate value overstatement concerns utmost good faith.
- 2014 Q27 is an irredeemable bond D. Q34 ex-dividend leaves the current dividend with the vendor B, supported by retrieved SEC guidance.
- 2014 Q29 describes paid-up capital, absent from the options. Called-up means demanded, not necessarily paid. Q40 describes utility, also absent. The online portal version of Q40 has different options including utility; those were not substituted for the supplied scan.
- 2015 Q6 underbilling ₦2,000 at ₦200 requires an additional debit of ₦1,800. Q31’s three-per-cent payment within nine days is 3/9; net 30, A. Q41 applies the stated ten-per-cent depreciation: ₦14,450 × 90% = ₦13,005, C. Q42 working capital is ₦2,150 + ₦700 + ₦100 − ₦1,800 = ₦1,150.
- 2016 Q18 planning C, Q31 interest C and Q49 email local-part/domain separation A correct their printed keys. Q35 omits certainty from the assurance distinction and is a defective-option item.
- 2014 Q48 accepts hybrid, digital and mainframe configurations under “can.” The retrieved AMS publisher-deposited abstract establishes historical hybrid meteorological simulation; ECMWF documents mainframe forecasting and NWS documents modern digital numerical models. This does not claim routine operational hybrid forecasting today or a full-text AMS download.
- 2016 Q39 preserves printed B/D and accepts both historical school listing requirements. The original historical SSM listing instrument was not retrieved; the compound key and school-context limitation are explicit, and the rules are not presented as current exchange requirements.
