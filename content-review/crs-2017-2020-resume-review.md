### crs-2019-q34

C. Africa's non-participation in God's purpose

### crs-2019-q4

Customs and religious practices codified as a system of law in Israel before Moses received the Ten Commandments are called

B. Judaism
C. Torah
D. Talmud

Printed key: **B**. Recommendation: **Defective premise**.

The options conflate religion, law/teaching, later rabbinic discussion and a Babylonian code. None fully matches a codified Israelite law system in the stated pre-Sinai chronology. Keep the original terms and explain the mismatch.

## Additional linked wording decision: CRS 2020 Q29

In his Sermon on the Mount, Jesus taught Christians to

A. turn the other cheek to their enemies
B. be merciful to their enemies
C. love and pray for their enemies
D. do good to their enemies

Printed key: **C**. The unfinished draft scores C alone; the older 2005 counterpart scores A/C/D. Matthew 5:39 gives the other-cheek instruction. KJV Matthew 5:44 explicitly includes love, doing good and prayer; RSV omits the longer doing-good wording. The broad stem does not name a translation or a particular verse. My recommendation is A/C/D for this broad wording, preserving C as the source answer. This is an explicit passage overlap, not an extra answer inferred from an unrelated theological truth.
