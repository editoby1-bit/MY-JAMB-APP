## Precision and limits

Topic lessons distinguish facts from broad historical examination conventions. They do not make nearest numerical options exact, treat all debentures as convertible, equate general crossing with account-payee restriction, or call every storage device a processor. Assurance certainty and uncertain timing are distinguished. No virus-removal procedure is fabricated from an unsuitable offered option.

This covers topics tested in the twelve supplied papers, not a complete official-syllabus audit. Source-era institutional names and simplified historical accounting/legal rules remain qualified. A compiled answer key is not represented as a verified official JAMB marking scheme. The per-question mapping audit is coverage-map.tsv.

No application, shared backend or first-400 source-data files were edited by the coverage helper.
