# Commerce 2017–2020: Key Points coverage review

## Source scope

Each of the four supplied papers contains 40 numbered source questions across two PDF pages. The new batch therefore has 160 source items, and the combined bank has 960. No Q41–Q50 records were invented for these papers. The compiled key must be compared only with question occurrences actually present in the supplied scans.

Only 2017 Q1 asks for the physical examination booklet type. It uses the existing introductory mapping as a stable navigation fallback and adds no invented Commerce curriculum content. The first questions in 2018, 2019 and 2020 are substantive Commerce questions. In 2020 the occupation-classification question occurs twice, as Q1 and Q5; both authentic occurrences are retained.

## Coverage result

The expanded map contains 960 entries across the existing 54 lessons. No new lesson was needed: the new questions revisit tested points already covered by the focused 2001–2016 lessons. All original 800 mappings remain unchanged, and every paragraph of all earlier lessons is retained. All lessons remain 300–450 words and include a worked example and Common traps.

The four final year JSONs are individually checked against their mapped lessons before integration. `coverage-map.tsv` records the new 160 mappings and question stems.

## Precision checks

- Terms of trade use export prices relative to import prices, not the merchandise trade-balance total. The shortened export-ratio wording receives its necessary explanation.
- Historical authorized-capital, issued-capital and share-count calculations are mapped to their exact finance lessons. The lessons distinguish nominal capital, issue proceeds, unissued capital, reserved capital, called-up amounts and actual paid-up amounts. A missing paid-up option is not redefined as called-up capital.
- Insurance lessons distinguish covered indemnity loss, fidelity cover, subrogation, utmost good faith, reinsurance and endowment benefits. A burglary policy is not automatically fire cover.
- Money functions, recognition, legal backing, relative scarcity and token value remain distinct. Legal prohibition of forgery does not make counterfeiting physically impossible.
- Association labels are explained by actual membership and arrangement. Cooperative business groups, cartels, syndicates, consortiums and mergers are not interchangeable solely because each involves cooperation.
- Proper waste disposal and safe products may satisfy both a specific environmental or quality duty and wider social responsibility. The corresponding lessons preserve these overlaps without inventing a false contradiction.
- Communication and transport connect businesses in different ways. The map follows each question's specific function, and distinguishes advice, delivery, storage and statement-of-account records.
- Product utility, product mix, market segmentation and pricing skimming are separate concepts. A satisfaction question missing utility is not taught as an exact definition of product mix.
- Virus removal is taught through appropriate malware detection, quarantine or disinfection, rather than calling destructive formatting the exact process of cleaning an existing program.
- Technology classification distinguishes input/processing/output hardware, storage media, cache, markup language, operating systems, browsers, protocols and passwords.

## Small supplements to existing lessons

No existing material was removed. Contextual additions make cybercafé operating costs explicit, including electricity, connectivity, equipment, maintenance and staff. The lesson does not claim one cost is the universal leading problem in every location or period. A short reminder is also retained in the network lesson.

The advertising lesson now explicitly explains billboard local reach and the absence of a universally best medium. The management lesson explains why a simple line structure can suit a small enterprise while preserving qualifications about size, specialist needs and complexity. Both additions support repeated questions rather than manufacturing new topics.

## Limits

These lessons cover material tested in the supplied 2001–2020 sources. They are not claimed to complete an official Commerce syllabus. An independent syllabus audit should identify any remaining gaps. Administrative instructions and duplicate source occurrences do not create extra curriculum coverage merely by increasing the source-record count.
