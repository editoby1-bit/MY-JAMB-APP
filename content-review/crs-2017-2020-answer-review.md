# CRS 2017–2020: completed answer and coverage review

All four uploaded papers contain 40 questions, giving 160 new source records. The release brings CRS to 960 past-paper records plus 50 existing starter questions: 1,010 in total. The earlier 850 records and their scoring remain unchanged, including CRS 2003 Q3: C only.

[Independent final data audit](crs-2017-2020-final-audit.md) · [Final coverage audit](crs-2017-2020-final-coverage.md)

## Owner decisions applied

- **2019 Q15:** B, forty years, retained with a clear warning that Scripture does not directly state Joshua’s Exodus age. Caleb’s forty-year age is not passed off as Joshua’s.
- **2019 Q33:** C only. The concise explanation identifies political prominence as the non-goal, without an extended discussion of D.
- **2020 Q29:** C remains the booklet’s primary answer; A and D also score. Matthew 5:39 and KJV 5:44 directly supply the instructions. The note explains the translation difference and the Luke parallel.

- **3 direct textual key corrections:** 2018 Q10 A; 2018 Q33 B; 2020 Q34 A.

- **4 qualified single answers:** 2019 Q12 B, Q15 B, Q29 C and Q34 D. Their contextual or evidential limits are explicit.
- The 13 original pending flags in this uploaded batch are closed. Older interpretive-review queues remain separate; this does not claim every earlier disputed ruling has been settled.

### crs-2017-q10

According to Peter, the reward of the elders who tend the flock of God is

A. an everlasting inheritance

C. prosperity
D. immorality

Printed key: **B**. Accepted: **B**.

The printed “unfading crown of God” is repaired to “unfading crown of glory,” the unmistakable wording of 1Peter5:4; option order and intended B remain.

Reference: 1 Peter 5:1–4

### crs-2017-q29

### crs-2020-q29

In his Sermon on the Mount, Jesus taught Christians to

A. turn the other cheek to their enemies
B. be merciful to their enemies
C. love and pray for their enemies
D. do good to their enemies

Printed key: **C**. Accepted: **C/A/D**.

A, C and D are accepted under this broad stem. Matthew 5:39 contains the other-cheek instruction; KJV Matthew 5:44 includes love, doing good and prayer. C remains the booklet’s intended answer. B’s explicit enemy-mercy wording is in Luke’s parallel sermon.

Reference: Matthew 5:43–44

### crs-2020-q34

A. sober
B. prayerful
C. watchful
D. holy

Printed key: **C**. Accepted: **A**.

1 Thessalonians 5:6 explicitly says to keep awake and be sober. A therefore completes the quotation, while the booklet’s C repeats the first idea instead.

### crs-2020-q39

According to John, the word Rabboni in Hebrew means

A. disciple
B. priest
C. master
D. Teacher

Printed key: **D**. Accepted: **D/C**.

D, Teacher, and C, Master, are accepted. John 20:16 is glossed Master in KJV and Teacher in RSV/modern translations; the older English Master here is an address to a teacher, not a different identity.

Reference: John 20:16
