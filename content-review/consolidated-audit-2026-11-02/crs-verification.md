## Five proposed moves: exact-stem assessment

1. **CRS-19 / crs-2005-q36 — Jerusalem's destruction**: currently D plus C. Keep. The unrestricted “a consequence of” accepts immediate political cause (2 Kings 24:20–25:1: Zedekiah rebelled) and the theological cause (2 Chronicles 36:14–19: persistent transgression and rejection of messengers). The report's own evidence explicitly supports both. Making D sole would require a qualifier such as “immediate political cause,” which is absent.
2. **CRS-36 / crs-2013-q9 — Egyptian servant guides David**: currently C plus A. Hold. 1 Samuel 30:11–15 directly records both the provision of food/water and his conditional oath not to kill or return him. Kindness versus fear as the decisive motive is interpretive. The text does not explicitly state “he guided David because David was kind.” Do not present either motive as uniquely proved.
3. **CRS-37 / crs-2013-q21 — Amos's occupation**: currently A plus C. Hold; recommend discussing A-only for consistency with the newer Amos item rather than silently applying. Amos 7:14–15 explicitly says herdman and gatherer/dresser of sycamore fruit. A is the direct named occupation; C is a broader category supported by the agricultural work. The uploaded MOVE ruling itself says “both are accepted.” This is a policy choice about broad occupational descriptions, not a factual contradiction that this report settles.
4. **CRS-44 / crs-2015-q24 — most important Josiah reform**: currently D plus A. Hold. 2 Kings 22:8–13 supplies discovery of law book; 23:1–3 supplies covenant renewal. Neither passage ranks the two with the question's subjective “most important.” The report recommends A while its own evidence admits no unique ranking. Cannot responsibly change D to A alone.
5. **CRS-48 / crs-2016-q13 — true vine**: currently B plus C. B is the exact phrase in John 15:6 (“cast forth”). C “taken away” directly occurs at 15:2 about an unfruitful branch and also ordinarily paraphrases removal. **Potential single-best correction, but not sufficiently certain for unilateral removal under the user's joint-decision rule.** If owner chooses strict clause matching, B alone with a trap explaining verse 2 is coherent.
