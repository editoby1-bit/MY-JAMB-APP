# CRS — reconciled audit of 53 flagged questions

**Status:** Consolidated academic recommendations, **not authorization to change scoring**. All 53 entries are preserved from the source first pass, with later corrections integrated. Historical Bible translations and official JAMB keys have not been fully authenticated.

## Decision summary

| Classification | Count |
|---|---:|
| KEEP_MULTIPLE | 42 |

## Implementation safeguards

1. Preserve existing scoring until historical wording, translation, and question-ID matching are checked.
2. `DEFECTIVE` means editorial rewrite, not automatic deletion of accepted answers.
3. `MOVE_TO_TRAPS` and `REMOVE_SPECIAL` are recommendations, not automatic changes to `alsoAccept`.
4. This report supersedes both the CRS first pass and the targeted corrections for these 53 entries.

### CRS-01 — crs-2001-q21 (2001)

**Question:** What acts of righteousness did Isaiah admonish his people to demonstrate?

A. Helping the poor and offering sacrifices  
B. Learning to do good and seeking justice  
C. Defending the orphans and respecting the widows  
D. Avoiding evil and praying for others

**Existing accepted:** B, C  
**Ruling:** `KEEP_MULTIPLE` — Retain existing accepted answers.  
**Evidence:** Isaiah tells the people to cease evil, learn to do good and seek justice. He also names practical defence of the fatherless and the widow. The key's answer quotes one pair exactly, but the following verse also supports the option about protecting these vulnerable groups.  
**Status:** No immediate change recommended.
