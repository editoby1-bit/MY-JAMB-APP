# Commerce — Reconciled Audit of 54 Flagged Questions (v2)

**Scope:** Consolidates the complete first-pass Commerce audit with the eight-case targeted verification dated 2026-11-02. All 54 original question entries are reproduced below; revised rulings supersede earlier ones. These are independent editorial assessments, not authenticated JAMB answers.

## Reconciled classification counts

- **KEEP_MULTIPLE**: 40
- **MOVE_TO_TRAPS**: 8
- **REMOVE_SPECIAL**: 0
- **DEFECTIVE**: 4
- **NEEDS_REVIEW**: 2

**Total: 54.** The classification counts include conditional recommendations; they do **not** mean all scoring changes are approved. In particular, the two Second-Tier Securities Market questions still require historical evidence, and defective items require editing. No app code has been changed.

## Implementation safeguards

- Match the **original question ID, exact wording, and option text** before any modification.
- **KEEP_MULTIPLE:** retain genuinely valid accepted answers.
- **MOVE_TO_TRAPS:** recommend one best answer, but do not revoke previously accepted credit until the relevant evidence and editorial sign-off are recorded. For `commerce-2006-q44`, the capital-employed convention is specifically conditional.
- **DEFECTIVE:** preserve existing scoring until a corrected question is approved; the recommended answer field below may describe a *provisional* interpretation, not a ready-to-apply change.
- **NEEDS_REVIEW:** do not implement an answer-key change without historical source verification.
- Cross-check repeated questions (`commerce-2006-q24`/`commerce-2014-q48`; `commerce-2010-q33`/`commerce-2016-q39`) to avoid inconsistent treatment.

### 16. commerce-2006-q44 — MOVE_TO_TRAPS

**Question:** XYZ Balance Sheet As At 31/12/2004 (₦):
Share capital 200,000; Reserves 100,000; Long-term liabilities 40,000; Current liabilities 30,000; Total 370,000.

A represents shareholders’ funds. This classification is conditional on confirming the intended convention; do not remove existing credit without editorial approval.

**Implementation:** Retain existing scoring pending editorial repair; do not silently remove accepted answers.
