## Independent disputed-item checks

The final classification avoids calling a valid printed answer false merely because a narrower alternative exists. 2009 Q37 retains printed D (brokers) and also accepts B (jobbers), distinguishing broad market-professional usage from technical principal dealing. Q44 retains offer/acceptance and accepts price/goods under the vague sale-contract wording. Q50 accepts both social and environmental responsibility for proper waste disposal. 2012 Q18 accepts the three direct communication benefits: reduced travel costs/risk, connecting people, and facilitating delivery. Root's context-sensitive decisions on 2011 Q7 and Q26 preserve the source answer while accepting other directly recognized limitations.

For 2010 Q24, the auditor independently read the year reviewer's retrieved WTO 2011 Nigeria Trade Policy Review source (`online-2010/wto2011-extracted.txt`, Tourism section). It explicitly says the Tourism Master Plan was published in 2006, and describes poor infrastructure and undeveloped facilities. This contradicts literal lack of a plan before the 2010 paper and supports C, inadequate developed tourist facilities/centres, as the best offered choice. The explanation does not claim Nigeria lacked natural attractions; the same source expressly lists numerous attractions. This is an evidence-backed correction, not an unsupported alternative preference.

2011 Q29 is qualified to creditors' voluntary liquidation. The reviewed CAMA sections distinguish members appointing in general meeting from creditors' nomination priority; the source's unqualified directors answer is not taught as a universal rule. Root selected B as the best listed context-dependent answer, not a claim that every voluntary liquidation excludes members.

Separate year-review files contain the actual institutional-source research performed by the paper reviewers. This audit does not claim a second independent download of every cited web page or an official JAMB marking scheme.
