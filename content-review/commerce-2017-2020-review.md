# Commerce 2017–2020: source and answer review

Four supplied papers, 40 original questions each. This adds 160 paper items to the published 2001–2016 bank. Original printed keys, wording and option counts are preserved, including missing-option defects. No official JAMB marking scheme was established.

New-paper academic classifications: {'corrected': 5, 'multiple': 11, 'noanswer': 3, 'misprint': 8, 'debatable': 22}; plus 1 preserved administrative booklet-identification instruction. All questions have a prepared offline Teach Me and a Key Points mapping. The combined bank has 54 lessons and 187 reviewed repeat groups (517 occurrences). These lessons cover the imported topics; this is not a claim of a completed full-syllabus audit.

- Only 2017 Q1 is an administrative booklet-identification instruction in this batch. It has no universal answer, credits any attempt, and is excluded from defective-question study counts and fast games. Q1 in 2018–2020 is substantive. All four papers and original year keys end cleanly at 40; no missing ten questions were invented.
- Commerce 2005 Q39 and Commerce 2007 Q26 have only three printed options. No fourth option was invented.
- The 2005 Q39 compiled key says C/D, but no D is supplied. C is accepted and the source-key defect is explained.
- Source typos and conflicting figures are retained and explained; historical institutional/legal references are taught in context.
- Corrected answers enter Wrong Answers in Circulation; genuine multiple and missing-correct-option cases enter the corresponding Tricky Questions category. Only real distractor errors become Exam Traps. Repeated tested points retain their separate year occurrences.
- Fast games exclude multiple/no-correct-option questions. Defective-option questions credit any attempt without painting an offered answer factually correct.
- Published CRS metadata and content remain preserved; no shared backend configuration changes. The 45-second content download timeout remains.

### commerce-2017-q2

Country’s terms of trade are said to improve when the ratio of her export

A. decreases
B. remains constant
C. increases
D. equals import

Printed key: **C**. Treatment: **misprint**. Accepted: **C**.

Terms of trade improve when export prices rise relative to import prices, so the intended ratio increases. The printed stem omits the complete phrase export prices divided by import prices; C is accepted with that missing context explained.

### commerce-2017-q9

Marketing skimming is an example of

A. market penetration
B. sales promotion
C. pricing policy
D. advertising

Printed key: **C**. Treatment: **misprint**. Accepted: **C**.

Price skimming is a pricing policy that begins with a relatively high price, often for a new differentiated product, and may reduce the price as the market develops. The source says “Marketing skimming,” intending price or market skimming.

### commerce-2017-q12

A public company with an authorized capital of 60,000, issued 36,000 shares at 150k each. What is its capital?

A. ₦60,000
B. ₦36,000
C. ₦54,000
D. ₦90,000

Printed key: **A**. Treatment: **debatable**. Accepted: **A**.

Retain A for the explicitly stated authorized capital of ₦60,000. The final phrase its capital does not specify a different capital measure. Issuing 36,000 shares at ₦1.50 would raise ₦54,000, but that is issue proceeds rather than automatically the stated authorized ceiling.

### commerce-2017-q14

An association of voluntary organizations that work together for a common aim while retaining their independence is a ___.

A. syndicate
B. merger
C. cartel
D. consortium

Printed key: **C**. Treatment: **debatable**. Accepted: **C**.

C, cartel, is the intended business-combination label in the source. A cartel involves otherwise independent enterprises cooperating over a common objective, conventionally market or competitive arrangements. The stem is broad and omits those usual distinguishing features; it should not be taught as a complete exclusive legal definition.

### commerce-2017-q15

The social responsibility factor of an organization is geared towards

A. contributing to the sustenance and development of its host community
B. operating without disrupting the very essence of the environment
C. tackling the socio-economic problems of the state
D. meeting the needs and demands of the shareholders

Printed key: **A**. Treatment: **multiple**. Accepted: **A, B, C**.

Community development, environmental protection and addressing wider socio-economic problems are all recognized social-responsibility aims. The stem gives no restriction selecting only the host community. Retain source A and also accept B and C; meeting shareholders’ demands alone does not express the wider social purpose.

### commerce-2017-q22

If the rate of turnover of a company in 1999 was 4 times while the average stock was ₦49,600, determine the turnover.

### commerce-2017-q23

The characteristic which ensures that money is free from forgery is it’s

A. legality
B. durability
C. recognition
D. credibility

Printed key: **C**. Treatment: **multiple**. Accepted: **C, A**.

Recognition is the supplied answer: recognizable authentic features help people distinguish genuine money from counterfeit items. ### commerce-2017-q26

### commerce-2017-q31

Part payments made on allotted shares by subscribers is usually the ___.

B. authorized capital
C. issued capital
D. called-up capital

Printed key: **D**. Treatment: **noanswer**. Accepted: **No fully correct option; credit any attempt**.

Money actually paid by subscribers on allotted shares is paid-up capital, which is absent from the options. Called-up capital is the amount demanded for payment, not necessarily the amount received. The major source of income to commercial banks is?

B. deposits
C. interest
D. overdrafts

Printed key: **B**. Treatment: **corrected**. Accepted: **C**.

Interest earned on lending is income. Deposits are liabilities owed to customers, while loans and overdrafts are facilities on which interest may be earned.

### commerce-2020-q40

An organization which focuses on consumer satisfaction is practicing ___.

A. consumerism
B. market segmentation
C. selling concept
D. marketing concept

Printed key: **A**. Treatment: **corrected**. Accepted: **D**.

The marketing concept starts with customers’ needs and seeks satisfaction through a coordinated offering. The selling concept emphasizes persuading buyers to take existing output. Segmentation divides a market into groups; consumerism concerns protection of consumer interests. None replaces the organization-wide customer orientation defined here.
