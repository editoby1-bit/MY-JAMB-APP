- 2017 Q34: stock C and debentures D are both capital-market instruments. The printed treasury-bill A is short term and incorrect here. The item appears both under multiple-answer questions and Wrong Answers in Circulation; the wrong-key annotation must show both accepted alternatives.
- 2019 Q22: irredeemable bond D leaves principal without a scheduled redemption; the supplied redeemable-bond C contradicts that distinction.
- 2020 Q38: boosting members' trade B is the central trade-association objective, rather than the supplied victimization answer D.
- 2020 Q40: a business focused on consumer satisfaction applies the marketing concept D, not consumerism A.
- 2018 Q40: formatting is not disinfection of an existing program. No antivirus/disinfection option is supplied; this repeats the earlier defective wording.
- 2020 Q12: utility is absent from the supplied options, all of which are different “mix” labels. Online occurrences with different options were not substituted for the actual scan.
- 2018 Q23/Q24 explicitly state five million shares at 50k, without the earlier paper's currency symbol before the share count. Authorized capital is ₦2.5 million and the unissued 20% is ₦500,000. The loose use of “reserve” is explained rather than replacing the original wording.
