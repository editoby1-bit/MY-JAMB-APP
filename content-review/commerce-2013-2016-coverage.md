## Precision checks

The new labour lesson distinguishes manual or blue-collar work from skill level. Physical effort does not by itself mean unskilled labour. The production lesson preserves the distinction between construction and factory manufacturing, including ambiguous assembly stems.

The insurance lesson explicitly teaches the average-clause formula, the difference between sum insured, value at risk and loss, and the contractual nature of life-assurance benefits. Traditional “consolation” language is not confused with an ex gratia payment. The missing certainty term in assurance questions is explained.

Company documents separate memorandum provisions from internal articles and a public prospectus. Called-up capital is demanded; paid-up capital is received. A question offering called-up capital for actual part payment is not silently taught as an exact definition. Old authorized-capital terms remain historically qualified.

Contract forms distinguish a court record from a deed or simple contract. A lien retains possession without automatically conferring ownership. Wrong-buyer delivery is not performance; frustration requires its legal conditions rather than any accident.

Product benefits distinguish core, actual/formal and augmented benefits. Utility and satisfaction are not defined as product mix. Segmentation is not the same as differentiation, skimming or penetration pricing.

The securities lesson distinguishes cum dividend and ex dividend, contract notes, government securities, irredeemable borrowing and historical settlement postponement charges. Historical 10% public offering and minimum-100-shareholder rules are presented as old school SSM requirements, not permanent current listing rules.

Office-record lessons explicitly work the ₦2,000 versus ₦200 undercharge: the adjustment is ₦1,800. Postal services distinguish poste restante, courier, registration, posting evidence and delivery evidence. Communication forms distinguish verbal/non-verbal from oral/written.

Stored data may be retrieved from more than one suitable medium. Downloading has a direction; generic transfer is broader. A database supports management information but is not by itself the entire management information system.

Professional institutions are not credited with unlimited statutory powers. Legal professional discipline is distinguished from a simplified association label. NCC consumer engagement is taught as consumer protection and feedback, not merely provider profit.

## Earlier-lesson supplements

Existing paragraphs were retained. Short additions supply the terms-of-trade price-index ratio, poste restante/courier terms, the stock-turnover formula with cost-versus-sales qualification, direct business communication benefits, international commercial cooperation and forward/backward integration versus syndicate/project arrangements. Each resulting old lesson remains within 450 words.

## Limits

These lessons cover the material tested in the supplied 2001–2016 papers. They are not presented as a completed official Commerce syllabus. A later syllabus audit should identify gaps independently. No earlier source question or year occurrence was removed, and no lesson was added simply to manufacture curriculum coverage for an administrative instruction.

`coverage-map.tsv` records each new question’s mapping for root integration review.
